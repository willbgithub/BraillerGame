CAVE GAME
	- You are walking through a cave trying to escape. There's fire chasing you.
	- You are asked to type a braille character by its letter being spoken to you. If you get it wrong, you must wait for a second before trying again.
	- If you get it right, you progress forwards.
	- Pressing F1 will show the correct braille character on screen, as well as play the index audibly.
	- After getting a letter correct three times, a sound will play, indicating that it will no longer provide assistance when you use F1.
	- If you solve the character without pressing F1, it counts as getting it correct twice.
	- After getting every letter correct at least four times, you escape.
